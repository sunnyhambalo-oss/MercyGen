MercyGen

Following God’s call in Kitwe

Terry & Denise Page are planting a church with a team of dynamic young leaders in Kitwe, Zambia. This mission is rooted in disciple-making, leadership development, and a deep love for the presence of God.

We are focused on forming disciples and raising leaders, while depending upon the power of the Holy Spirit for every step of the journey. Our heart is to see people transformed, empowered, and mobilized for the work of the Kingdom.

The younger generations are a key focus of what we do, because we believe they carry the future of the church and must be raised in faith, purpose, and bold obedience to Christ.
